MAMAPA

Pravila privatnosti

Zadnje ažurirano: 16. listopada 2026.

Mamapa Zagreb je neprofitna zajednička karta mjesta prilagođenih roditeljima s malom djecom. Vodi je privatna osoba (kontakt: watanabeayano17@gmail.com).

Koje podatke prikupljamo

Kad se prijaviš Google računom, primamo tvoje ime, e-mail adresu i profilnu sliku. Spremamo mjesta, recenzije i fotografije koje sam objaviš. Uz recenziju se javno prikazuje samo tvoje ime; e-mail adresa nikad nije javna.

Fotografije

Fotografije se prije spremanja smanjuju, a podaci o lokaciji snimanja (GPS) uklanjaju se. Molimo ne objavljuj lica djece.

Gdje se podaci čuvaju

Podaci se čuvaju na servisu Supabase u EU (Irska). Stranica se poslužuje preko Cloudflarea. Podatke ne prodajemo i ne koristimo za oglase.

Tvoja prava

Možeš u bilo kojem trenutku obrisati svoje recenzije i mjesta u aplikaciji. Za brisanje računa ili pristup svojim podacima javi se na watanabeayano17@gmail.com i to ćemo napraviti u roku od 30 dana (GDPR).

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

Mamapa Zagreb is a non-commercial community map of places that work for parents with young children. It is run by a private individual (contact: watanabeayano17@gmail.com).

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We store the places, reviews and photos you post. Only your first name is shown publicly next to a review; your email address is never public.

Photos

Photos are resized before they are stored, and the location data (GPS) inside them is removed. Please don't post children's faces.

Where data is kept

Data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland). The site is served through Cloudflare. We don't sell data or use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can delete your own reviews and places in the app at any time. To delete your account or get a copy of your data, email watanabeayano17@gmail.com and we will do it within 30 days (GDPR).
